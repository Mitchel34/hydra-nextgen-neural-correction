Important remaining research requirements include qualified native lifecycle and component identity, conservation and unit checks, causal feature availability, full state/replay fidelity, leakage-safe fitting and stacking, fixed-support comparison against strong outlet baselines, and complete resource/evidence accounting. Passing a generated engineering fixture cannot replace those requirements.

The research reforecast is a constructed, idealized-state NextGen product. It must not be described as archived operational NextGen forecasts. Prior research-exposed periods cannot be silently relabeled as untouched holdout data.

This snapshot does not transfer private operational permissions, campaign budgets, runtime configurations or data access. It includes no executable route into the private campaign.
